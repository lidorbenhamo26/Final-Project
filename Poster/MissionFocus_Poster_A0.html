Lidor Ben Hamo & Yahli Rapaport
Advisor: Dr. Moshe Sulamy  ·  Department of Software Engineering & Information Systems
01

Background & Need

Executive-function assessment still relies on questionnaires (BRIEF-A) and traditional lab-style tests:

  • Stressful & repetitive — scores may reflect motivation, not true ability
  • Low engagement, especially for young users
  • Subjective ratings — no objective behavioral data
The need: measurement that is objective, repeatable — and actually fun to take.
02

The Solution

Mission Focus turns assessment into a 10-minute spaceship mission.

Instead of asking the participant to describe behavior after the fact, the game observes behavior while it happens.

  • More engaging — the player completes a mission, not a dry test
  • Objective measurement — reactions, errors, choices and timing are logged automatically
  • More realistic behavior — the player handles tasks, distractions and priorities inside one dynamic environment
  • Assessor-ready output — the session becomes an executive-function profile + CSV
Why it works: the player feels inside a mission, while the system quietly turns behavior into measurable data.
03

Core Capabilities

  • Playable 10-minute mission — game-based, not a quiz
  • Measures key executive functions — attention, memory, inhibition & planning
  • Logs behavior automatically — reactions, errors, choices and timing
  • Generates assessor outputs — HTML report + CSV
  • Keeps data local — privacy by design
04

How It Works

1

Participant Onboarding

Basic details are entered before the mission

2

Interactive Tutorial

The player learns movement and station controls

3

10-Minute Mission

Tasks appear across 4 stations while the player chooses priorities

4

Background Logging

Reactions, errors, choices and timing are recorded silently

5

Assessor Report

Executive-function profile + CSV, generated instantly

07

Design Challenges

  1. Fun vs. valid data — the mission feels playful, while console tasks stay precisely timed
  2. Invisible measurement — the player focuses on the game while behavior is logged silently
  3. Modular task design — new cognitive tasks can be added without rebuilding the system
  4. 3D asset integration — AI-generated props needed Unity cleanup and adaptation
08
