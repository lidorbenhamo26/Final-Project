01

The Problem

Diagnosing ADHD still leans on subjective questionnaires (BRIEF-A) and repetitive lab-style computer tests - methods that rarely capture how attention really behaves in the real world.

Why it falls short
Stressful & easy to fake
Scores reflect effort, not true attention
No objective behavioral data
02

The Solution

A 10-minute spaceship mission that watches behavior as it happens:

Engaging

Players fly a real mission, not a dry questionnaire

Objective

Every action, error and timing is logged

Real-world

Handles distractions & competing priorities

Assessor-ready

Outputs a profile + CSV instantly

03

Inside the Mission

RADAR SCANSustained Attention

Measures: Pickup latency · Wiring errors · Completion time
